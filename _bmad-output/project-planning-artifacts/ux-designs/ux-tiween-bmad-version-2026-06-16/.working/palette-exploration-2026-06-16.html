🎨 Tiween — Palette Exploration

Five complete dark-theme palettes, judged on the real discovery surface (EventCard + chrome), not abstract squares. Action accent is #F8EB06 yellow throughout (brand equity — kept). Focus of this run: warm the neutrals (cool-clinical surfaces → warm-cultural), pushing the range from safe to daring. Open in a browser; compare the feel of the field + how posters and yellow sit on it.
