🎨 Tiween — Light Palettes

Six light directions. Light flips the brand story: posters stop glowing (cinema) and start sitting on a page (editorial / festival programme) — which, for a read-only directory, may be more on-message than dark. Three rules light forces, designed-around below: (1) yellow #F8EB06 is invisible on white (1.1:1) → action flexes to a dark fill; yellow becomes a highlight/seasoning only. (2) posters need a subtle frame or they look pasted-on. (3) this contradicts the locked dark-first decision — a real pivot, not a tweak. Each shows EventCard + chrome + error, with the dark-teal equity carried as the action/ink color.

Verdict: The clever hybrid — the card itself is a dark teal "well" (posters glow, yellow lives there) inside an otherwise light/editorial page. Resolves light's biggest weakness (posters look pasted-on) AND keeps yellow. Most complex to build, but arguably the most Tiween-true way to go light.

Sally's read — light: ① Gallery Paper = most "cultural institution", teal ink keeps equity. ③ Mint Field = the truest "light Tiween" (keeps the green family). ⑥ Paper + Dark Wells = the smartest hybrid — light page, glowing posters, yellow survives. ② Crisp Gallery finally lets real cobalt work as a link. ④ Newsprint & ⑤ Sunwash are the boldest repositions (editorial-press / sunny-warm).

Big-picture flag: any light theme is a pivot away from the locked dark-first decision — it changes the brand from "cinema at night" to "festival programme by day". For a read-only directory that's defensible, even strong. But it's a stakeholder-level call, not a token tweak. If one of these sings, the right next move is a head-to-head: your favourite light vs your favourite dark (the aubergine?) on a full screen — then decide the brand's daylight question deliberately.
