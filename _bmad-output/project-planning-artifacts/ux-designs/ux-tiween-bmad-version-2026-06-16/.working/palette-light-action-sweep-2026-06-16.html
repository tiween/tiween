Sally’s read: For a light, editorial directory I’d shortlist ① Coral (warmest welcome, modern-cultural) and ② Terracotta (most Tunisian, grown-up). ③ Electric Teal is the safest brand move — the action color is literally Tiween green, so “we changed yellow” becomes “we promoted our own hue”. ④ Magenta is the boldest/most ownable if you want Tiween to feel like a festival brand. ⑤ Amber is the gentle off-ramp from yellow.

Note every action here also passes contrast as a fill under white text (coral/terracotta/magenta/teal-dark all ≥ 4.5:1; amber uses dark text on the chip + a darker fill #B87708 on the CTA).
