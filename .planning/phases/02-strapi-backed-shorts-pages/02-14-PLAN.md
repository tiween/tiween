<objective>
Remove the account-language field from the client's profile and account-email flows, per the owner decision on UAT gap G-02-5 (2026-11-10): the browser decides the site language, the field leaves user accounts entirely, and emails use the request locale (else fr).

Purpose: FR56. Today the reset and email-change emails only localise through the stored account language because the client posts `{ email }` alone (deferred-work DW-67, DW-74); once the field is gone, sending the page locale is what keeps those emails in the visitor's language. The Strapi override already reads body.locale for both routes.
Output: profile form without the language select, locale on the forgot-password and change-email requests, the dead presentational language selector and the profile.language key removed.
</objective>
