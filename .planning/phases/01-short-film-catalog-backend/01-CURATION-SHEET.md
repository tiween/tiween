## Criteria used (D-16, D-19)

A film is eligible when ALL of these hold:

1. **Tunisian link:** Tunisian (co-)production or Tunisian director.
2. **Duration:** about 40 minutes or less (the data test accepts at most 45; 41-45 would be flagged here; no film is over 30).
3. **Availability:** legally watchable online through an official channel or platform (the director, producer, distributor, festival, broadcaster or a licensed platform; never a re-upload), OR festival-selected/awarded, then listed with a trailer only.

A film is **importable** (D-19 readiness) when it also has a **director who is a person**, and **a watch source** (streaming link or trailer). A poster is NOT required for import: the launch importer publishes posterless films through the logged D-20 bypass and the client placeholder covers them (Phase 2/4).

## How to read the table

- **Official link:** the exact URL the film would be watched at, with the channel owner named and why that owner counts as official. The owner of every YouTube channel was read from YouTube's own page data, not inferred from the video title.
- **Poster source:** `none` means no real 2:3 poster was found on a festival, distributor or director page. The importer derives a 16:9 backdrop from the official YouTube thumbnail (D-12); the mock catalog's "posters" were only those 16:9 thumbnails or dead artify.tn image paths, so they are not posters.
- **Distinctions:** mapped to the enum from the source's own wording (selected, nominated, winner, special-mention, honorable-mention, grand-prize). A claim with no source URL does not enter the data file.
- **Translations:** French is the source. AR and EN are AI-drafted unless stated; the Arabic title is the film's Arabic-script original title where that was confirmed (D-13). The owner reviews the Arabic in the admin after import. A film with no verifiable synopsis has none in the data file rather than the mock's generic text.
- **Recommendation:** `include`, `exclude`, or `needs owner input` (what is missing is stated in Notes; such films are NOT in the data file).
- Language codes (`aeb` = Tunisian Arabic) are carried from the mock catalog where no source contradicts them; none of the sources state the spoken dialect explicitly. Durations are those of the watchable video file, rounded to the minute.

## Method and limits

Channel owner, duration and upload date were read from YouTube's own page data for every video. Facts then come from the cited press, film-database, festival and platform pages, each fetched and read. The general web search services (DuckDuckGo, Bing, Mojeek) answered with captcha or irrelevant pages in plan 01-12, so new candidates were found through YouTube search and the cinematunisien film database (JCC 2024 and 2014 programmes and palmarès), then checked on the film's own YouTube page data and its cinematunisien page. Absence of a distinction on the sheet means none was found, not that none exists. Plan 01-12 kept to its caps (8 candidates) but used about 4 to 6 page reads per candidate; the YouTube page-data reads replaced several manual fetches.
