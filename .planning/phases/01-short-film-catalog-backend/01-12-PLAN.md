Research uses the web (search and fetch). Every fact on the sheet carries a source URL; a fact that cannot be verified is written as "unverified" and never enters the data file as truth.

Batch cap and research budget (these keep the plan inside its context budget): at most 8 new candidates are researched in this plan. Each one gets at most 2 web searches and 4 page fetches, and each fetch asks a targeted question (director, duration, year, channel owner, distinctions, poster) rather than for the whole page. A candidate whose director, duration or official source is still unverified at the end of its budget is recorded as needs owner input or excluded, and it still counts toward the 8. Leads noticed beyond the cap go to `## Leads for later curation` (title, where found, one URL) without research.
