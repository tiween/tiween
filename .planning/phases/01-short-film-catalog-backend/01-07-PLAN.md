<objective>
Make the existing events-manager Works form reachable and make its media fields real (SDATA-02, FR57). Research found that `WorksListPage` and `WorkFormPage` are routed nowhere and that `MediaInput` is a URL-paste stub that fabricates ids, so nothing is ever uploaded. This plan routes the pages, adds the navigation entry, and replaces the stub with Strapi's media-library dialog, which uploads through the configured upload provider (ImageKit when its keys are set).

Purpose: every admin-entry requirement in this phase (new short-film fields in 01-08, locale tabs and publish bar in 01-09) needs a reachable form whose media are real uploads.

Output: routes + side-nav item + translations, a media-library-backed `MediaInput`, and jsdom tests for both.
</objective>
