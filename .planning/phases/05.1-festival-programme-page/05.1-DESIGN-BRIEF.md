# Festival programme page — confirmed design brief

## 1. Job and audience

- Tunisian culture-goers 18–34, on a phone, often between screenings during the festival or planning tonight's slot. Arrive from festival links, social posts and film pages.
- Surface mode: **Operate** (find what's on, where, when). Scan speed over spectacle.
- Launch festival: **Journées Cinématographiques de Carthage (JCC)**, an Arab-African film festival in Tunis. Arabic titles are common, so RTL and mixed-script handling are core.

## 2. Outcome and proof

Under a minute to answer: _what's on today / next?_, _what's on at this venue?_, _where and when does this film play?_

The data model already supports it: `event-group` (type `festival`) has `events`. Each event has a `venue`, `startDateTime`, `eventStatus` and ordered `screenings`. Each screening has `movie` (creative-work: film or short-film), `order`, `videoFormat`, `audio/subtitleLanguage` and `features`.

## 3. Direction

- Visual authority: Gold Leaf × Aubergine (`apps/client/DESIGN.md`, now global). No new visual world.
- Structure: compact festival header (poster, name, dates, venue count), then a three-way view switch (**By date**, the default; **By venue**; **By film**) with a shared day strip.
- Precedents in the designer's screens (`_bmad-output/project-planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-tiween-bmad-version-2026-06-16/imports/`):
  - `TIWEEN_Film_Mobile.png` "Choisir une séance": day tabs, then venue cards with showtime tiles → the **By film** view.
  - `TIWEEN_Room_Cinema_Mobile.png` "Les films projetés": day tabs, then film cards → the **By venue** view.
- Focal moment: the **programme card**.
  - Header: time, venue, format, section label.
  - Body: the films as a **visible ordered list** (poster thumb, title, director, runtime), then the total runtime.
  - Shorts-only programmes say so in the header (e.g. "Programme courts · 5 films · 82 min"). When a feature is present it is listed last and weighted as the main film.
  - **Departure from the inherited design:** the room screen hides a programme's films behind tabs. A visible list lets all of them be compared at once.

## 4. Scope and boundaries

- In: one route `/[locale]/festivals/[slug]`; film festivals only; the three views; "Au festival" screenings linking to the film detail page.
- Out:
  - festivals index;
  - theatre and music festivals;
  - ratings and reviews;
  - **ticketing**: no "Réserver", no seat flow; showtime tiles carry no purchase action and open the programme;
  - Netflix-style carousels;
  - mock data presented as real;
  - hiding films behind tabs.

## 5. States and ranges (JCC estimates; verify against the real programme before mockups)

- 1–10 days (JCC about 8); 1–15 venues; 0–80 programmes per day; 1–8 films per programme.
- During the festival: opens on today, scrolled to the next screening. Ended screenings collapse under "Déjà projetés".
- Before the festival: opens on day 1. After it: a full archive with no "today".
- Empty day: empty state suggesting the next day with screenings.
- Cancelled or postponed: icon plus text label, never color alone.
- Missing poster: reserved stripe artwork.
- Loading: skeleton programme cards. Errors: translated codes (`t('errors.<CODE>')`).

## 6. Interaction and layout

- **By date:** time-ordered programme cards; a "Prochaines séances" jump; venue and section filter chips (needed at JCC density).
- **By venue:** one section per venue (name, address, map link) listing that day's programmes.
- **By film:** poster rows; each film shows all its screenings grouped by day and venue, plus the programme it belongs to. A short screened twice in different programmes shows both.
- Selected view, day and filters are kept in the URL, so a shared link opens the same view. The day strip scroll-snaps and works on touch. Targets ≥ 44px. Full RTL mirroring.
- Desktop: the view switch moves into a sticky sidebar; venue sections sit side by side.

## 7. Owner decisions and constraints

- **Competition sections: yes.**
  - Sections are per festival (e.g. Compétition officielle longs / courts, Carthage Ciné-Promesse, Panorama, Hors compétition).
  - New data in the **events-manager plugin** (plugin-first): a festival-section belonging to an event-group, with a localized name and a competition / non-competition kind; events point to one section.
  - Shown on the programme card as a small trophy-outline glyph plus text (not gold; gold is for actions). Section filter chip in the date and venue views; the section is shown per film in the film view.
- **Film team present: yes.** Reuse the screening `features` vocabulary (category `event`, "équipe présente"); shown as ✲ plus "Équipe du film présente".
- Reuse: `DateSelector`, `EventVenueFilter`, `VenueCard`, `EventCard` (base for the programme card), `FilmCard`, `Map`, `ShareDialog`, `EmptyState`.
- Dates through `lib/dates.ts`; times `15h00` in FR and `15:00` in AR/EN; Western digits.
- WCAG 2.1 AA; axe-core clean in FR/AR/EN.
- Build gates: this phase's `DESIGN.md` + `EXPERIENCE.md` + owner-approved mockups before any code.
